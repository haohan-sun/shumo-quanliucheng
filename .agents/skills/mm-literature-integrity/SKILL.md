# MM Literature Integrity

Check that each selected citation exists, supports the attributed claim, and has a traceable locator
and verification status. Distinguish direct evidence from inference. Record unresolved conflicts;
do not fabricate bibliographic fields or use a snippet as proof of a stronger claim.
