# MM Evidence Retrieval

Collect only evidence within the approved scope. Preserve locator, retrieval time, verification
status, intended use, and limitations in Evidence Passport-compatible records. Separate facts,
human assumptions, estimates, and experimental results. Return records to the orchestrator; do not
fabricate citations, reuse restricted assets, or decide the final route.
